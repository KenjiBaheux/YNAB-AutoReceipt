Welcome to YNAB Receipt Porter

1. Connect to YNAB

This application runs entirely in your browser. All receipt images, tokens, and data stay on your computer. We pinky swear 🤙.

To get one, go to your YNAB account Settings > Developer Settings > Personal Access Tokens, or click here to read YNAB's guide.

2. Select Default Budget

Choose the YNAB budget you want to import receipts into by default.

3. Select Default Account

Choose your default account. You can easily switch this later on the main view.
